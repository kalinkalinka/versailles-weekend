Day one

Saturday

Leave home around noon. Lunch inside the château orbit, then claim the hotel and wander the Trianon estate until golden hour.

Lunch

Ore — Ducasse

Contemporary café in Pavillon Dufour with courtyard views. No château ticket required for Ore itself; book ahead for a smoother welcome. Perfect bridge between Paris and Trianon.

Ore site +33 1 30 84 12 96
Afternoon

Domaine de Trianon

Petit Trianon → Sofia Coppola Marie-Antoinette exhibition → Grand Trianon → Queen’s Hamlet. Soft paths, pink marble, and the hamlet’s storybook cottages — all before dinner.

~12:00

Leave Paris

From République / Filles du Calvaire / Oberkampf → RER C or Metro + RER toward Versailles. Leave ~11:00 to make the 12:30 Ore lunch at Place d’Armes.

12:30–14:00 ✓ booked

Lunch at Ore–Ducasse

Pavillon Dufour, 1st floor. Seasonal French café lunch with château views — reservation confirmed for 2 (Luka Meng). Leave time to walk to the hotel for 15:00 check-in.

15:00

Check-in · Trianon Palace

Waldorf Astoria Versailles – Trianon Palace, 1 Boulevard de la Reine. Drop bags, freshen up.

~15:15–16:00

Rest & settle

Short reset in the room — change shoes, hydrate, then head out to the Trianon estate.

~16:00–18:30

Trianon estate

Petit Trianon + Sofia Coppola Marie-Antoinette exhibition → Grand Trianon → Queen’s Hamlet. DUO card — walk in, no timed ticket. Soft afternoon light before dinner.

19:30

Dinner · La Véranda

Brasserie at Trianon Palace — zero travel after Trianon. Apply the ~$100 F&B credit once to this meal. Dinner served 19:00–22:30. Phone-only booking: reserve at the front desk at check-in or call 01 30 84 55 55.

Day two

Sunday

Breakfast on us (Diamond), VR at 09:00, Palace with DUO priority entry, lunch near the Palace, a proper rest at the hotel, then Musical Fountains before the Neptune finale.

Morning

Breakfast & VR

Hotel breakfast included (served 7:00–10:30). Then VR Light of Liberty at 09:00 (€7/person, Pavillon d’Orléans) — Palace right after with DUO card priority entry, no timed ticket.

Midday

Palace

State Apartments & Hall of Mirrors, straight in after the VR. Out by ~12:00 — lunch is a short stroll away.

Midday

Sunday lunch — two contenders

Angelina inside the Palace (Pavillon d’Orléans) or La Flottille on the Grand Canal. Both open Sundays — details below, pick your vibe.

07:30–8:30

Breakfast at the hotel

Included with Hilton Diamond (served 7:00–10:30). Early start, unhurried — leave ~8:45 for the Palace.

09:00

VR · Light of Liberty

VR-only ticket (€7/person) at the Pavillon d’Orléans — flexible entry, aim for 09:00. Buy in advance online or at the door. ~15 min experience plus check-in and headset fitting.

9:20–12:00

Palace visit

DUO card priority entry straight after the VR — no timed ticket needed. State Apartments & Hall of Mirrors; aim to be out by noon.

~12:15–13:30

Lunch near the Palace

Angelina inside the Palace (Pavillon d’Orléans) or La Flottille on the Grand Canal — see the two contenders below.

~13:45–16:00

Back to the hotel · rest

20–25 min walk or a short taxi. Proper room rest — this is what the late checkout is for. Check out ~16:00; bags held by the concierge.

16:00–17:30

Musical Fountains

Grandes Eaux Musicales via the Neptune gate at the hotel’s doorstep — see the dedicated Sunday 18 Oct 2026 schedule below.

17:20 → home

Neptune finale → RER

Historical jets 17:20–17:30 — the day’s highlight reel — then RER back to Paris.

Sunday lunch — two contenders

Both open Sundays. Angelina wins on zero travel; La Flottille wins on canal-side charm before the fountains.

Option A · Inside the Palace

Angelina

Legendary salon de thé on the 1st floor of the Pavillon d’Orléans — the same building as the VR. Famous hot chocolate and Mont-Blanc, plus savory salads, quiches and sandwiches. Zero travel: finish the Palace visit and sit down, inside the ticketed area.

Open Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00 (last access 17:30) · Reservations 01 39 20 08 32

Official page 01 39 20 08 32
Option B · Grand Canal

La Flottille

Classic French brasserie-restaurant on the Grand Canal, in the park — 1900s décor, canal-side terrace. Lunch here flows straight into the Musical Fountains afternoon.

Open daily, 9:00–18:30 · Reservations +33 (0)1 39 51 41 58

laflottille.fr 01 39 51 41 58

  1. 1 · ~16:00 — Walk from Trianon Palace toward the gardens (Neptune / Dragon gate is the natural approach from Boulevard de la Reine).
