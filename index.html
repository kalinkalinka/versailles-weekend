Overview

The locked plan

Everything below is set except Saturday dinner — that’s your pick. Scroll the dinner section and tell me which vibe wins.

  • Who: 2 adults, Paris residents (EEA ticket rates)
  • Hotel: Waldorf Astoria Versailles – Trianon Palace, Sat night only, check-in 15:00

Day one

Saturday

Leave home around noon. Lunch inside the château orbit, then claim the hotel and wander the Trianon estate until golden hour.

Lunch

Ore — Ducasse

Contemporary café in Pavillon Dufour with courtyard views. No château ticket required for Ore itself; book ahead for a smoother welcome. Perfect bridge between Paris and Trianon.

Ore site+33 1 30 84 12 96
Afternoon

Domaine de Trianon

Petit Trianon → Sofia Coppola Marie-Antoinette exhibition → Grand Trianon → Queen’s Hamlet. Soft paths, pink marble, and the hamlet’s storybook cottages — all before dinner.

~12:00

Leave Paris

From République / Filles du Calvaire / Oberkampf → RER C or Metro + RER toward Versailles. Aim for Place d’Armes / Ore before the rush.

~13:00

Lunch at Ore–Ducasse

Pavillon Dufour, 1st floor. Seasonal French café lunch with château views.

15:00

Check-in · Trianon Palace

Waldorf Astoria Versailles – Trianon Palace, 1 Boulevard de la Reine. Drop bags, freshen up.

15:30+

Trianon estate

Petit Trianon + Coppola exhibition + Grand Trianon + Hamlet. Estate of Trianon typically opens from midday; confirm timed tickets.

Evening

Saturday dinner — your choice

See Dinner picks below. Gordon Ramsay is on-site (7-course Sat only); the rest are short hops into town.

Day two

Sunday

Breakfast on us (Diamond), VR first, then the State Apartments, a credit-backed lunch at La Véranda, and Musical Fountains before late checkout.

Morning

Breakfast & VR

Hotel breakfast included. Then VR Light of Liberty at 10:30 or 11:00 — book the slot with official ticketing.

Midday

Palace

State Apartments & Hall of Mirrors with Passport access. Pace yourselves — lunch is waiting back at the hotel.

Afternoon

La Véranda → Fountains

Apply the ~$100 F&B credit once here, then Musical Fountains in the gardens before late checkout.
